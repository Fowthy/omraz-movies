SELFLESS - THE STORY

ACT 1: THE MIRROR LABYRINTH

An elongated humanoid figure wanders through an infinite corridor of mirrors. Cold silver-blue surfaces stretch in all directions, reflections multiplying into distant darkness. The figure searches, looking into each mirror passed, seeking something—perhaps themselves.

At first, the reflections show the figure correctly. But then something changes. One mirror shows not the figure's face, but glimpses of an ochre organic world—warm sepia tones bleeding through cold silver glass. Soon every mirror reveals this other place: vast organic architecture, cathedral-like arches of flesh and membrane, beautiful and inviting.

One mirror glows with particular warmth, showing a peaceful landscape beyond. The figure is drawn toward it, reaching out. When their hand touches the glass, it ripples like liquid. They step through, crossing between worlds.

ACT 2: SEDUCTION

The figure emerges into a vast organic cathedral. Membrane arches tower overhead, tissue columns rise in the distance. Everything glows with warm ochre light. It is beautiful beyond imagination—architecture made of living flesh, yet peaceful and inviting.

The figure explores with wonder, reaching up to touch the impossible structures, dwarfed by the immensity. But as they wander deeper, enchanted by the beauty, the environment subtly shifts. Organic structures orient toward the visitor. The space tightens. The mirror doorway grows distant, then disappears entirely.

The trap closes gently. The figure is surrounded by flesh architecture with no exit visible. The beautiful prison is complete.

ACT 3: TRANSFORMATION

Something feels wrong. The figure examines their own hand and sees the edges beginning to blur, fingertips becoming slightly translucent. The dissolution has begun.

During calm moments, the transformation is slow—edges softening, form becoming gradually translucent. But when the environment grows aggressive, the change accelerates violently. Flesh unravels into fibrous threads. The figure arches in pain as their body splits and dissolves, becoming translucent with the environment visible through gaps.

The organic architecture responds with excitement, consuming the shed flesh, walls growing with incorporated material. There is no escape—every path is blocked, the prison revealed.

The figure finds a mirror embedded in the organic wall and rushes toward it with desperate hope. But as they reach for it, the transformation surges, racing up their arms. Their body betrays the escape attempt.

The dissolution continues relentlessly. Half-dissolved becomes hollow silhouette. Solid flesh becomes threads and wisps. The figure's distinctive posture remains, but they are becoming something else entirely—fading, translucent, merging with the environment that consumed them.

Then the figure discovers another mirror floating in the void. Different from the others. Special. They approach with slow deliberate steps and look into its surface.

THE REVELATION: The mirror shows the original fleshed self trapped INSIDE the glass. The transformation was not destruction—it was exchange. The warm flesh is now imprisoned in cold reflection, pressing against the glass from within, screaming silently. The figure standing outside is what replaced them. Watcher became watched. Outside became inside.

ACT 4: THE REALIZATION

Two versions of the same person, eternally separated. The trapped self pounds against the glass barrier, desperate to escape. The fading figure outside reaches toward the mirror in horror and desperation.

Hands press against glass from both sides—fading hand from outside, flesh hand from inside—same gesture, opposite sides of the barrier. They cannot connect. They cannot reunite.

The fading figure claws at the mirror desperately, but the effort only accelerates its own destruction. Threads unravel from the strain of reaching. The attempt to reunite is destroying what remains.

Still, the figure keeps reaching. The environment churns around them, disturbed by the destruction of what it had absorbed. The fading form presses its translucent face against the glass, trying to be closer to its trapped self, features dissolving from the pressure.

ACT 5: SHATTERING

The figure unravels completely. Starting from the reaching hand, threads and wisps explode outward in beautiful destruction. The body dissolves into countless pieces—the cost of yearning for what was lost.

From inside the mirror, the trapped fleshed self watches in grief and horror, witnessing their own death from within the prison. Tears stream down the face pressed against glass.

The organic architecture absorbs the floating fragments, consuming the shattered pieces, reclaiming what it made. The environment grows with the new material.

In the end, only the mirror remains—floating alone in the vast organic void. The trapped self is still visible inside, pressing against glass eternally. The surrounding architecture pulses slowly, satisfied.

FINAL IMAGE: The mirror floats in an infinite organic cathedral void. Within it, the trapped elongated humanoid self is visible, small, pressing against glass eternally. Vast ochre flesh architecture surrounds it, watching, breathing. The Other is victorious forever.

Eternal imprisonment complete.
