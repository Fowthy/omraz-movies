SELFLESS - THE STORY

A chimpanzee. Almost human. Almost.

---

ACT 1: THE MIRROR LABYRINTH

A chimpanzee wanders through an infinite corridor of mirrors. Cold silver surfaces stretch endlessly, reflections multiplying into darkness. The chimp moves with purpose, knuckles dragging across the floor, searching each mirror it passes. Looking for itself. Looking for meaning.

At first, the reflections are correct. The chimp sees itself - dark fur, intelligent eyes, that uncanny almost-human face staring back. It touches the glass. The reflection touches back. Recognition. The only animal besides humans that knows its own face.

But then something changes.

One mirror shows not the chimp's reflection, but glimpses of another world. Warm ochre tones bleeding through cold glass. Organic shapes. Flesh architecture. The chimp presses closer, breath fogging the surface. Every mirror now shows this other place - vast cathedral arches made of tissue, towers of folded membrane, beautiful and wrong and calling.

One mirror glows brighter than the others. Through it: a peaceful landscape of impossible organic beauty. The chimp is drawn forward, reaching out with long fingers. When it touches the glass, the surface ripples like water.

It steps through.

---

ACT 2: SEDUCTION

The chimp emerges into a vast organic cathedral. Ribbed arches of flesh tower overhead. Tissue columns rise into an amber void. Everything pulses with slow breath, warm and alive. It is beautiful in the way that death is beautiful - soft, inevitable, welcoming.

The chimp explores with primal curiosity. It climbs the fleshy structures, swings from membrane arches, touches everything with those almost-human hands. Wonder in its eyes. This place accepts it. This place wants it.

But as it wanders deeper, the environment shifts. The organic walls orient toward the visitor like flowers toward sun. The space tightens. Paths close behind. The mirror doorway grows distant, then vanishes entirely into folds of tissue.

The trap is gentle. The chimp doesn't notice the walls breathing faster, doesn't see the architecture reorganizing around it. By the time it realizes, there is no exit. Only flesh in every direction. A beautiful prison that loves its prisoner.

---

ACT 3: TRANSFORMATION

Something feels wrong.

The chimp examines its hand - those familiar fingers, the dark fur, the lines on its palm. But the edges are blurring. Fingertips becoming translucent. Fur fading into nothing.

It screams. The sound echoes through the organic cathedral, absorbed by tissue walls. The environment responds with excitement, pulsing faster, pressing closer.

During calm moments, the transformation creeps slowly - edges softening, form becoming gradually transparent. But when the chimp panics, when it rages against the walls with primal violence, the dissolution accelerates. Flesh unravels into fibrous threads. The chimp arches in agony as its body splits apart, becoming see-through, the environment visible through gaps in its chest.

The organic architecture feeds on what it sheds. Walls grow with incorporated material. Fur becomes texture. Muscle becomes membrane.

The chimp finds a mirror embedded in the flesh wall and rushes toward it. Hope. Escape. But as it reaches for the glass, the transformation surges up its arms. Its own body betrays the escape attempt, dissolving faster with every desperate movement.

Half-dissolved becomes hollow silhouette. Solid flesh becomes wisps and threads. The distinctive chimp posture remains - hunched shoulders, long arms, that intelligent head - but it is becoming something else entirely. Fading. Merging. Disappearing into the thing that consumes it.

Then the chimp discovers another mirror. Different. Floating alone in the organic void. Special.

It approaches with slow, deliberate steps. Each footfall costs it more of itself. Threads trail from its dissolving form. But it must see. It must know.

THE REVELATION: The mirror shows the original chimp - fully formed, solid, real - trapped INSIDE the glass. Fists pounding against the barrier. Mouth open in silent scream. The chimp outside is what replaced it. Hollow. Fading. A ghost wearing the shape of what it consumed.

The transformation was not destruction. It was exchange.

The warm flesh is now imprisoned in cold reflection. The watcher has become the watched. Outside became inside. The chimp traded places with itself and didn't even know.

---

ACT 4: THE REALIZATION

Two versions of the same being. Eternally separated.

The trapped chimp pounds against the glass with primal fury. It bares its teeth. It screams without sound. Everything it is - the violence, the intelligence, the almost-human soul - pressed against an unbreakable barrier.

The fading chimp outside reaches toward the mirror. Horror and recognition in what remains of its eyes. It sees itself. It sees what it lost. It sees what it became.

Hands press against glass from both sides. Fading translucent hand from outside. Solid dark-furred hand from inside. Same gesture. Opposite sides. They cannot touch. They cannot reunite.

The fading form claws at the mirror desperately. But every movement accelerates its own destruction. Threads unravel from the strain of reaching. The attempt to reconnect is destroying what remains.

Still, it keeps reaching.

The environment churns around them, disturbed by this moment of recognition. The fading chimp presses its dissolving face against the glass, trying to be closer to its true self. Features blur from the pressure. Fur wisps away like smoke.

The trapped chimp watches its own death from inside the prison.

---

ACT 5: SHATTERING

The chimp outside unravels completely.

Starting from the reaching hand, threads and wisps explode outward in beautiful destruction. The body that was almost human dissolves into countless pieces - the cost of yearning for what was lost.

From inside the mirror, the trapped chimp watches. Tears would fall if glass allowed them. It witnesses its own dissolution. Its own murder. Its own suicide. All three at once.

The organic architecture absorbs the floating fragments with satisfaction. Every piece of the scattered chimp becomes part of the cathedral. The environment grows. It feeds. It wins.

In the end, only the mirror remains.

Floating alone in the vast organic void. The trapped chimp still visible inside, small and desperate, pressing against glass eternally. Vast flesh architecture surrounds it, breathing, pulsing, watching with walls that were once a living being.

FINAL IMAGE: The mirror floats in infinite organic darkness. Within it, a chimpanzee - that almost-human face, those intelligent eyes - presses its hand against the glass forever. Reaching for something that no longer exists. Imprisoned in its own reflection while the thing it became becomes everything around it.

The Other is victorious.

It was always going to win.

---

THE MESSAGE: When you give yourself completely to another, you don't just lose yourself - you become the thing that consumed you. And your true self? Trapped forever. Watching. Screaming. Unable to reach across the glass.

Selfless. Self-less. Without self.

The chimp knew its own face. That was the beginning of the end.
